# Reference study

All six supplied concepts were inspected at their original size. Their artwork, logos, token symbols, portraits and brand marks are not used in Mama Wallet.

**0ae8d29cd2d083645f980a24702bfe2d.webp.** Borrow the oversized balance with quieter decimals, joined pill action bar with a circular centre action, and floating rounded navigation. Change the composition to a warm, readable family wallet with pine and mint, named navigation, and a distinct approval strip instead of investment cards.

**33ab16cdcdd3bb613e11e68fb036952c.webp.** Borrow the comfortable numeric keypad, restrained row separators, and review breakdown. Change burgundy to pine, increase Elder controls to 60px, and separate the pressure question from amount entry so each step has one decision. No portraits or exchange dashboard are reused.

**6249bc779c296136cb176fdb86b2f152.webp.** Borrow the strong amount hierarchy, quick-amount chips and deliberate bottom action. Change dark/red to the specified calm palette. A receive QR is omitted because the mock API supplies no deposit address; no token logos or fictional deposit instructions are introduced.

**c10741b7c138c8339113721c99379931.webp.** Borrow the airy onboarding scene with floating utility symbols and a single pill CTA, plus softly separated in-app alerts. Use original inline shield, wallet and phone drawings. Replace push permissions with explanations of the existing family protection and in-app updates.

**original-2c28161948d17c6fdd4c5114f32ab2c4.webp.** Borrow atmospheric pastel surfaces, clearly separated payment actions and readable recent-activity rows. Change the crypto portfolio into a money balance with a visible demo disclosure; use the spec’s mist gradient and warm Elder surface instead of the reference palette and asset cards.

**original-ee7514749cfefc5476cb788367895ad2.webp.** Borrow scalloped people/place avatars, a clear selected state and a dark rounded navigation tray. Use initials only, retain visible text on every navigation item, and replace social trading content with approved places and the parent’s device status.
